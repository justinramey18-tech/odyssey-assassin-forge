

# Custom Spell Creator -- Plain Language Plan

## What You're Getting

A "Create Your Own Spell" button inside your Arcana spellbook. You tap it, fill out a form (or let the AI design one for you), and your custom spell instantly appears everywhere -- in your spellbook, your Combat tab, and your Quick Actions menu. It works exactly like any official spell. The AI assistant also knows what time it is in the real world when helping you (4th Wall Time).

---

## How It Works (Big Picture)

Think of the app's spell list like a phone's contact list. Right now, it only has "built-in" contacts. This update adds a second list -- your personal custom contacts -- that sits right alongside the built-in ones. Whenever the app looks up a spell (to show it in combat, quick actions, etc.), it checks your custom list first, then the built-in list. This means your homebrew spells automatically show up everywhere without needing to rewire the whole app.

---

## What You'll See as a User

### 1. "Create Spell" Button in Your Spellbook
- A new button appears in the Arcana tab's spellbook view
- Tapping it opens a creation form

### 2. Spell Creation Form (3 Simple Steps)
- **Step 1 -- The Basics**: Name your spell, pick its level (cantrip through 9th), and choose a school (like Evocation or Necromancy)
- **Step 2 -- How It's Cast**: Set the casting time, range, duration, and what components it needs (verbal, somatic, material)
- **Step 3 -- What It Does**: Write the description, set damage/healing numbers, saving throw type, and what happens when cast at higher levels

You can fill out as much or as little as you want -- the AI can handle the rest.

### 3. AI Assist Button
- One tap and the AI generates a complete, balanced spell concept for you
- It considers D&D 5e balance rules so your spell isn't overpowered or underpowered
- It even writes the personality quips (Thunderhead, Jarvis, Deadpool voices) automatically
- 4th Wall Time stamps are included when that setting is on

### 4. Homebrew Badge
- Your custom spells get a small "Homebrew" label so you can tell them apart from official spells at a glance

### 5. Full Integration Everywhere
- **Spellbook**: Learn and prepare your custom spells just like official ones
- **Combat Tab**: Cast them during combat with full prompt generation
- **Quick Actions**: They appear in your Magic and Cantrips sections ready to quick-cast
- **AI Narration**: Combat prompts include your custom spell's details for rich storytelling

### 6. Edit and Delete
- Long-press (or tap-and-hold) any homebrew spell to edit or delete it
- Changes update everywhere instantly

---

## What Happens Behind the Scenes

### Saving Your Spells
- Custom spells are saved to your device's local storage (same way your character data is saved)
- They persist between sessions -- close the app, come back, they're still there

### Where Changes Are Needed
- **The spell lookup system** gets a small addition so it can find your custom spells alongside official ones -- this is the key change that makes everything else work automatically
- **The Arcana spellbook screen** gets the "Create Spell" button and the creation form
- **The spell card display** gets the "Homebrew" badge
- **The AI assistant** (the backend helper) learns how to generate spell concepts
- **The main app page** loads your custom spells on startup and makes them available app-wide

### What Stays the Same
- All existing spells are completely untouched
- The Combat tab, Quick Actions drawer, and spell casting flow don't need any rewiring -- they already know how to look up spells, and your custom spells will just be found naturally
- Your character data, abilities, Wild Shape, and everything else is unaffected

---

## 4th Wall Time

When you have 4th Wall Time enabled in settings, any AI-assisted spell creation includes the real-world timestamp. Combat prompts featuring your custom spells also carry this timestamp, keeping your AI narration time-aware.

---

## Summary

One new button, one creation form, one AI helper upgrade, and a small tweak to how spells are looked up. The result: unlimited custom spells that work everywhere in the app as if they were always there.
